## Design Context

### Users
20-40대 직장인 in the Toss ecosystem experiencing chronic sleep deprivation who want to quantify their sleep debt and discover practical recovery solutions. They're motivated by clear metrics and actionable plans, especially structured weekend catch-up sleep strategies, and need to see the numerical reality of their deficit to drive behavior change.

### Brand Personality
**Straightforward, Motivating, Data-driven.**  
Communicate sleep data with absolute clarity—no hedging, just numbers—and use warm, encouraging language around streaks and recovery wins. Align with Toss's direct, accessible tone: make the invisible (sleep debt) visible, then empower users with concrete next steps.

### Aesthetic Direction
TDS (Toss Design System) 준수. @toss/tds-mobile 컴포넌트 전용. 커스텀 미적 방향 없음.

### UX Principles
- **One-tap daily logging**: Minimize friction for 취침/기상 time entry to sustain daily check-in habit and streak continuity.
- **Transparent debt visualization**: Show cumulative sleep debt prominently at every screen using clear numbers and charts; make urgency immediate and quantified.
- **Reward clarity**: Gate recovery plan content behind rewarded ads explicitly; make the incentive structure transparent and frictionless.
- **Progressive engagement**: Start with simple numeric tracking; layer recovery plans, weekly reports, and personality tests as engagement deepens.
